Set it up in MetaTrader 5 (two charts)

Chart 1 — PANEL
Role = PANEL (default). The full-screen panel. It watches the symbols and is the only one that trades.
Chart 2 — BRIDGE
Role = BRIDGE. Talks to the AI and Telegram so the panel never freezes. Optional.
  1. Drag GTM AI Trade Core onto any chart, keep Role = PANEL, switch on Algo Trading.
  2. SETTINGS → + ADD / REMOVE SYMBOLS, then set the entry model of each symbol and the Account Guard.
  3. Watch it in SIMULATOR; switch to LIVE only when you are ready (it asks you to confirm).
  4. For AI and Telegram: Tools → Options → Expert Advisors → allow WebRequest for https://api.telegram.org and your AI service, then put GTM AI on a second chart with Role = BRIDGE, your AI key and bot token. Send /start to your bot.
